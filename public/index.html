    How to play

    Two teams of soldiers sit on a plane from x = −25 to 25, y = −15 to 15. On your turn, type a function and fire: the shot follows its graph starting from your soldier, killing every soldier it touches until it hits an obstacle or leaves the plane.

    Everyone sees the board from their own side, so you always fire to the right. The curve is shifted to pass through your soldier, so y = 0 fires straight ahead and y = x rises at 45°.

    Modes: y = f(x) · y′ = f(x, y) · y″ = f(x, y, y′) (starting with y′ = 0).

    Functions: sin cos tan asin acos atan sinh cosh tanh sqrt abs exp ln log sign floor, constants pi e, operators + − * / ^, implicit multiplication like 2x.

    Tip: steps like 5/(1+exp(-10(x+3))) jump over obstacles.
